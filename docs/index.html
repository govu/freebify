All the music. None of the ads.

Freebify is a desktop music player that streams original studio recordings — no ads between songs, no subscriptions, no account. Just the music.

Download for Windows GitHub ↗

Streaming made music free, then charged you in ads. Freebify removes the business model and keeps the player — every search returns the real studio version, every transition is instant, and nothing ever interrupts a song.

Everything inside.

Original recordingsSearch returns the real studio release — not a slowed edit, a sped-up rip or a cover with the same title.→
Zero adsNo audio interruptions, no banners, no “go premium” nags. The player plays. That’s the deal.→
Live synced lyricsLine-by-line karaoke that follows the song, auto-calibrated to the exact version you’re hearing.→
Genre radioStart a radio from any track and the queue stays on-vibe — the same sound, not the same song rehashed.→
Discord presenceYour status shows the song, the artist, the artwork and live progress — like the big players do.→
Silent auto-updatesNew versions download in the background and install when you quit. You never manage the app.→
Your libraryLikes, playlists, queue and history — stored locally on your machine, not our servers.→
No accountInstall and press play. No email, no login, no trial clock. That’s the entire signup flow.→
