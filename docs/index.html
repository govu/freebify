Why Freebify

Everything a music app
should be. Nothing it shouldn’t.

Built to feel premium and stay honest — the features you actually use, engineered to be fast and quiet.

Zero ads, period

No audio ads, no banners, no “upgrade to premium” nags. The player plays — that’s the whole deal.

Originals only

Real studio recordings — not slowed edits, sped-up rips or covers. What the artist released is what you hear.

Live synced lyrics

Karaoke-style lyrics that follow the song line by line, auto-synced to the right version — click any line to jump there.

Genre radio

Start a radio from any song and Freebify keeps playing what fits — the same vibe, endlessly, not the same track rehashed.

Discord presence

Show what you’re listening to on Discord — cover art, artist and live progress, just like the big players do.

Always up to date

The app updates itself silently in the background — every improvement lands without you lifting a finger.

Inside the app

Dark, calm, fast.

A deliberately neutral interface — nothing competes with the music. These are real captures, not mockups.
